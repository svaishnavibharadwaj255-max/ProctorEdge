04 / Computer vision

Camera-Based Proctoring

MediaPipe Face Mesh

Uses facial landmarks to estimate head pose and gaze-related indicators.

YOLOv8-Nano

Used for detecting objects such as phones and identifying additional people.

Frame Sampling

3 FPS

Approximately three sampled frames per second in the proposed pipeline.

Persistence Filter

3 sec

A 9-sampled-frame persistence rule helps filter brief detections.

Detection signals are indicators for human review, not proof of misconduct.

08 / Trust and evidence

Designed for Responsible Review

Timestamped Evidence

Snapshots can help examiners review when a flagged event occurred.

Persistence Filtering

Repeated detections can reduce alerts caused by very brief events.

Human Oversight

Automated indicators should support decisions, not replace examiner judgement.

Fairness

Lighting, camera placement and individual behaviour can affect detection results.

09 / Current limitations

What We Need to Improve

Vision Limitations

  • False-positive detections
  • Lighting and camera variations
  • Gaze estimation uncertainty

Assessment Limitations

  • Rule-based scoring is limited
  • Keyword checks can miss meaning
  • Contradiction checks can make mistakes
